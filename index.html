Interactive companion to the CSCM calibration report. The figures that are too dense to read on paper live here instead: step through them rather than squint at them.

Every parameter is held at its auto-generated CSCM default and one is moved at a time, on Test 1 at mesh1x. Pick a cell to see what that change does to the response and to the crack pattern. The default column is the same solve in every row.
